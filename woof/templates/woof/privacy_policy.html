{% blocktranslate trimmed %}1. Who we are{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}This Privacy Policy describes how{% endblocktranslate %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Woof Dogs{% endblocktranslate %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}(«Website», «we», «us») collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website{% endblocktranslate %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}https://woofdogs.world/{% endblocktranslate %}.

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}By using this Website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Woof Dogs is an informational website dedicated to dog breeds and related content. We do not provide user accounts, registration, or login functionality.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website is non-commercial: it has no advertising and no paid services, and we do not sell any data.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}2. Personal data you provide{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}In short: We only process the personal data you voluntarily provide.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website does not require registration and does not request usernames, passwords, or email addresses by default. However, if you contact us through the{% endblocktranslate %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Contact Form{% endblocktranslate %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}, we collect:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Email address — optional, only if you want a reply{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Message or inquiry content{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. Sending the form is your consent to this; the email is optional, and you can ask us to delete your message at any time.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your message is stored in the Website’s database, which only we can access. If you do not provide an email address, we cannot reply to you — your message is read and, where relevant, used to improve the Website. Please do not include sensitive personal data in your message.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}3. Automatically collected information{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website itself does not use trackers. Statistics are collected only if you accept analytics in the cookie notice; if you decline or ignore it, nothing is loaded.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}With your consent we load Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. It collects:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Pages visited and time spent on the site{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Browser type and version{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Device and operating system information{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Referring URLs{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Approximate location (country or city){% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We use these statistics only to understand how the Website is used and to improve it — never for advertising.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Like any web server, our server in Italy technically receives your IP address and browser details when a page is requested, in order to deliver the page and protect the Website against abuse. We do not use this to identify you.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}4. Purposes of processing{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Ensuring proper functioning of the Website{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Analyzing usage to improve content and structure{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Maintaining Website security and preventing abuse{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Replying to the messages you send us{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}5. Legal bases and regional laws{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website is operated from Italy, so your data is processed under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Italian data protection law.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Our legal bases are your consent (analytics, and the message you send through the contact form) and our legitimate interest in keeping the Website secure.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Analytics cookies and similar technologies are used only after you give your consent through the cookie notice on the Website. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Visitors from other countries{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}If you visit from outside the EU (for example the UK, the US, Canada or Australia), the same rules apply: we collect little, only with your consent, and never sell data.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}6. Cookies and tracking technologies{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website uses a few necessary cookies (language choice, form security). Analytics cookies are set only after you consent. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}More details in our{% endblocktranslate %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Cookie Policy{% endblocktranslate %}.

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}When you make a choice in the cookie notice (accept, decline, or close it), we record a small evidence entry in our consent register: a randomly generated consent ID, your choice, the notice version, and the timestamp (Article 7 GDPR). This entry contains no name, email, IP address, or other identifying information.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}7. Sharing of information{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only with Google (analytics, only with your consent) and with the hosting provider that runs our server, or where required by law.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}8. International data transfers{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Website’s data, including contact-form messages and the consent register, is stored in the Website’s database on a server in Italy.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Where data is transferred to Google LLC (based in the United States) for analytics, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified, and on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and Data Processing Addendum.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}9. Data retention{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Contact-form messages are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Consent-register entries are deleted after 12 months. Google Analytics data is kept for 2 months.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}10. Your privacy rights{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict your data.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also withdraw your consent at any time and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante).{% endblocktranslate %}
