{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Classification{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}We chose the FCI classification as a convenient basis: below are ten groups that bring together breeds of similar origin and purpose. The list is still incomplete — new breeds will be added.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The FCI groups dogs first of all by the work they were bred for. Sheepdogs herded flocks, hounds followed a trail, pointers helped hunt birds — the nature of this work shaped their build, coat and behaviour.

Within groups there are sections, which may differ by origin or coat type. Size, colour and other outward traits are taken into account in breed standards, but they do not determine the group. That is why a small and a large terrier can belong to one group, while dogs of similar size can belong to different ones.{% endblocktranslate %}
