{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Classification{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}We chose the FCI classification as a convenient basis: below are ten groups that bring together breeds of similar origin and purpose. The list is still incomplete — new breeds will be added.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The FCI sorts dogs first of all by the work they were bred for. Sheepdogs herded flocks, hounds followed a trail, pointers helped hunt birds — the nature of this work shaped their build, coat and behaviour.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Each group is divided into sections that refine the purpose, origin or traits of the breeds. Some sections are further divided into breeds with and without a working trial, as well as into subsections. We do not separate the latter in our classification.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Each recognized breed has an official standard, prepared by its country of origin and approved by the FCI, as well as its own number.{% endblocktranslate %}
